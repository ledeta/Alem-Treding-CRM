TRANSACTION MODAL COLUMNS:

CUSTOMERS PAGE Modal:
  1. Category        (from item.category)
  2. Item Name       (from item.name)
  3. Customer        (from selectedCustomer.name)
  4. Quantity        (from transaction.quantity)
  5. Selling Price   (from transaction.unitPrice)
  6. Total Amount    (from transaction.totalAmount)
  7. Sold By         (from transaction.createdBy.fullName)
  8. Branch          (from transaction.branch)
  9. Date            (from transaction.transactionDate)

ITEMS PAGE Modal:
  1. Item Name       (from item.name)
  2. Customer        (from customer.name) ← KEY: tx.customer?.name
  3. Quantity        (from transaction.quantity)
  4. Selling Price   (from transaction.unitPrice)
  5. Total Amount    (from transaction.totalAmount)
  6. Sold By         (from transaction.createdBy.fullName)
  7. Branch          (from transaction.branch)
  8. Date            (from transaction.transactionDate)
