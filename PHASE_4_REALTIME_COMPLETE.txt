🔄 REAL-TIME FEATURES ARCHITECTURE
────────────────────────────────────

Socket.io Namespaces (Separated for scalability):
  • /chat - Group conversations & direct messaging
  • /notifications - User notifications & alerts
  • /dashboard - KPI & business metrics updates
  • Default - General broadcast notifications

Event Types:
  Chat:
    ├─ join_conversation
    ├─ leave_conversation
    ├─ send_message
    ├─ edit_message
    ├─ delete_message
    ├─ mark_read
    ├─ mark_conversation_read
    ├─ typing
    ├─ get_active_users
    └─ direct_message

  Notifications:
    ├─ fetch_notifications
    ├─ mark_read
    ├─ mark_all_read
    ├─ delete_notification
    ├─ get_unread_count
    ├─ get_statistics
    └─ ping/pong

  Dashboard:
    ├─ get_dashboard_data
    ├─ kpi_update (broadcast)
    ├─ activities_update (broadcast)
    └─ requests_update (broadcast)
