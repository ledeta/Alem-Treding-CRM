                              TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

If you see "Invalid username or password":

1. HARD REFRESH the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R)
2. Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
3. Check username is lowercase: "admin" not "Admin"
4. Check passwords are EXACT (case-sensitive)
5. Make sure you're entering the RIGHT password for each step:
   - Step 1: Use password1 (Admin@2024! or Sales@2024!)
   - Step 2: Use password2 (AdminSecure#2024 or SalesSecure#2024)
