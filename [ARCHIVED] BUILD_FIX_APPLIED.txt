Build Error:

Root Cause:
  CASE SENSITIVITY ISSUE on Linux/Docker

Windows vs. Linux/Docker:
  • Windows: Case-insensitive (Charts === charts)
  • Linux/Docker: Case-sensitive (Charts ≠ charts)

The Actual Directory:
  📁 /components/charts  (lowercase 'c')

The Import:
  ❌ from '@/components/Charts'  (uppercase 'C')
